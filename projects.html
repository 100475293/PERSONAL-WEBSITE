2024San Diego State University

Home Assistant platform

A smart-home control layer that pulls separate IoT devices behind a single interface. Built with a team of five in an American university course, which in practice meant agreeing on scope with four people I had met two weeks earlier, in my second language.

My part was the device abstraction and keeping the integration surface small enough that adding a new device type did not mean touching the UI.

A nutrition tracking web app where I took the product owner role rather than a development one. The interesting problem was not the code, it was deciding what not to build: the backlog we started with was roughly three times what the team could ship in a semester.
